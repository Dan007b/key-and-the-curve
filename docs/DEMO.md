# Two-minute demo script

Setup: the desktop app full-screen, controller connected and calibrated, **Set level** pressed while holding it flat, sound on. **Watch demo** is the fallback if the controller misbehaves.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Level 1 card | "Phase Escape: a chase through a maze in curved space, with a fourth dimension." |
| 0:06 | Tilt the controller; the marble rolls | "I'm tilting a real controller, an ESP32 with an orientation sensor at 100 Hz. This is the hyperbolic plane: rooms crowd towards the edge because space grows exponentially." |
| 0:18 | A gold door blocks the way; turn the controller like a dial; the world turns gold; roll through | "The maze has a fourth dimension: five layers, five colours. Doors only open in their own colour. Turning the controller like a dial slides me through them. Even the music changes chord." |
| 0:32 | Level 2 (Rift): hold BOOT and tilt; the tesseract turns; the plank's gold edges fold away; it lies flat and becomes a bridge | "That tesseract is my view into the fourth dimension. This plank is four-dimensional, so I only see its shadow. Holding BOOT, I turn my view through 4D: the shadow slides, its fourth-dimension edges fold away, and once it lies flat, it's a real bridge." |
| 0:52 | Level 3 (Hunted): a red hunter, a line joins it to the marble, "1 red hunter can see you"; phase to green; it fades to a ghost | "Hunters only exist in one colour. This one is red, and so am I, so it can see me. Phase out and it's a ghost." |
| 1:05 | Level 4 (Curvature); twist jammed; roll a lap around the glowing pillar; the wheel turns under the needle; the world turns violet | "Here I can't dial at all. But I roll once around this pillar and come back violet." |
| 1:15 | The holonomy card with the two squares | "I carry a needle I never turn. In flat space a square has 90° corners, so I'd turn 360° and the needle would come back the same. Here the corners are 72°, so I turn 108° at each, 432° in all. I'm facing the same way, but the needle is 72° off: one colour. That's holonomy: in curved space, the path you take turns what you carry." |
| 1:38 | Grab a shard, the portal opens, roll in; stars | "Collect the shards, reach the portal, don't get caught three times." |
| 1:45 | The README | "Under the hood: exact Lorentz-matrix geometry in float64, GPU-instanced rendering at 60 FPS, and 140 tests, including a solver that proves every level is beatable and an autopilot that plays them all." |
